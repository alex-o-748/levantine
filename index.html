Vocabulary and texts are in Levantine Arabic (شامي). Definitions and word audio come from open knowledge sources: Wiktionary and Wikimedia Commons (Lingua Libre recordings by native speakers, CC BY‑SA). Sentence audio uses your browser's text-to-speech, which is Modern Standard Arabic flavoured — use it as an approximation and prefer the native recordings on single words when offered. The app respells dialect words before speaking them (so مرحبا isn't read with classical nunation) and can read ق as a hamza, the urban Levantine way. If your system has regional Arabic voices (Microsoft Edge ships ar‑LB / ar‑SY / ar‑JO ones), pick one above — they sound much closer to the dialect.
